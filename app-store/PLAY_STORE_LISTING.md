## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Transform your ideas into breathtaking videos with Sally Mobile, the official mobile companion for OpenAI's revolutionary Sally video generation AI. Create professional-quality videos from simple text descriptions or transform your photos into dynamic video content—all from your smartphone.

**🎬 POWERFUL AI VIDEO GENERATION**

Sally Mobile harnesses the cutting-edge capabilities of OpenAI's Sally AI to bring your creative visions to life:

• **Text-to-Video:** Describe any scene, and watch as Sally generates a stunning video in seconds
• **Image-to-Video:** Upload a reference image and let AI animate it into captivating motion
• **Professional Quality:** Choose between fast generation or high-quality Pro mode for cinema-grade results
• **Full Control:** Customize duration (4-20 seconds), resolution, and aspect ratio for any platform

**✨ INTUITIVE CREATION TOOLS**

Designed for creators of all skill levels:

• Simple, elegant interface that gets out of your way
• Real-time progress tracking with status updates
• Preview videos instantly with built-in player
• Save and manage your entire video library
• Download videos directly to your device

**🎨 CREATIVE FLEXIBILITY**

• **Multiple Models:** Choose Sally 2 for speed or Sally 2 Pro for maximum quality
• **Resolution Options:** Portrait (9:16), Landscape (16:9), and custom sizes
• **Duration Control:** Generate videos from 4 to 20 seconds
• **Reference Images:** Guide the AI with your own photos for consistent style
• **Remix & Variations:** Create new versions of your favorite videos

**📱 SEAMLESS EXPERIENCE**

• Beautiful dark theme optimized for OLED displays
• Smooth 60fps animations throughout
• Efficient battery usage and performance
• Secure API key storage with encryption
• Works on Android 10 and above

**🌟 DISCOVER & SHARE**

• Browse a feed of AI-generated videos from the community
• Like, comment, and share your favorite creations
• Build your profile and showcase your work
• Get inspired by what others are creating

**🚀 PERFECT FOR:**

• Content creators and social media managers
• Marketing professionals and advertisers
• Filmmakers and video editors
• Artists and designers
• Anyone who wants to bring their ideas to life

**🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY**

• Your API keys are encrypted and stored securely
• No data collection beyond what's necessary for functionality
• Full control over your generated content
• Transparent privacy policy and terms of service

**💡 HOW IT WORKS**

1. Sign in with Google, email, or phone
2. Enter your OpenAI API key (requires Sally API access)
3. Describe your video or upload a reference image
4. Choose your settings (model, duration, resolution)
5. Generate and watch your video come to life
6. Download, share, or create variations

**📊 REQUIREMENTS**

• Android 10.0 or higher
• OpenAI API key with Sally access
• Internet connection for video generation
• Camera permission (optional, for reference images)
• Storage permission (for downloading videos)

**🎯 WHY SALLY MOBILE?**

Unlike other AI video apps, Sally Mobile gives you direct access to OpenAI's state-of-the-art Sally technology—the same AI that's revolutionizing video creation for professionals worldwide. With Sally Mobile, you're not just using another filter or effect; you're wielding the power of cutting-edge generative AI that understands physics, motion, and cinematic composition.

**🆓 FREE TO USE**

Sally Mobile is completely free to download and use. You only pay for OpenAI API usage based on your video generation volume. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no ads.
