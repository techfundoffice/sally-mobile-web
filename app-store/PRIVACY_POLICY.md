# Privacy Policy for Sally Mobile

**Last Updated:** November 26, 2025  
**Effective Date:** November 26, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to Sally Mobile ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Sally Mobile application (the "App").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:

- **Email address** (if signing up with email)
- **Phone number** (if signing up with phone)
- **Google account information** (if signing up with Google OAuth)
  - Name
  - Email address
  - Profile picture (optional)

### 2. User-Generated Content

- **Video generation prompts** (text descriptions you provide)
- **Reference images** (photos you upload for image-to-video generation)
- **Generated videos** (AI-created videos from your prompts)
- **User interactions** (likes, comments, shares on the social feed)

### 3. API Credentials

- **OpenAI API key** (stored encrypted on your device)
  - We never transmit your API key to our servers
  - Stored securely using Expo SecureStore with encryption
  - Only used to communicate directly with OpenAI's API

### 4. Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information when you use the App:

- **Device information** (device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers)
- **App usage data** (features used, videos generated, time spent in app)
- **Performance data** (crash reports, error logs, app performance metrics)
- **Network information** (IP address, connection type)

### 5. Camera and Photo Library

With your permission, we access:

- **Camera** (to capture reference images for video generation)
- **Photo library** (to select existing photos as reference images)

We only access these when you explicitly choose to upload a reference image. We do not access your camera or photos without your permission.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### 1. To Provide and Maintain the Service

- Create and manage your account
- Process video generation requests
- Store and display your generated videos
- Enable social features (feed, likes, comments, shares)
- Provide customer support

### 2. To Improve the App

- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Fix bugs and technical issues
- Optimize app performance
- Develop new features based on user behavior

### 3. To Communicate With You

- Send important updates about the App
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Notify you of new features or changes
- Send security alerts if necessary

### 4. To Ensure Security

- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
- Protect against security threats
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Comply with legal obligations

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

### 1. With OpenAI

- Your video generation prompts and reference images are sent directly to OpenAI's Sally API
- This is necessary to generate videos using Sally AI
- OpenAI's use of your data is governed by their Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/privacy
- We use your API key to authenticate these requests on your behalf

### 2. With Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating the App:

- **Cloud hosting providers** (for app infrastructure)
- **Analytics services** (to understand app usage)
- **Crash reporting tools** (to identify and fix bugs)

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

### 3. For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to:

- Valid legal requests (subpoenas, court orders)
- Protection of our rights or property
- Investigation of fraud or security issues
- Protection of user safety or public safety

### 4. With Your Consent

We may share your information for other purposes with your explicit consent.

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage Location

- **User account data:** Stored on secure cloud servers
- **API keys:** Stored encrypted on your device only (never on our servers)
- **Generated videos:** Stored on cloud servers with secure access controls
- **Usage data:** Stored on secure analytics platforms

### Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption in transit:** All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Encryption at rest:** Sensitive data (including API keys) is encrypted when stored
- **Access controls:** Strict access controls limit who can access user data
- **Regular security audits:** We regularly review and update our security practices
- **Secure authentication:** We use OAuth 2.0 for third-party authentication

### Data Retention

- **Account data:** Retained until you delete your account
- **Generated videos:** Retained until you delete them
- **Usage data:** Retained for up to 2 years for analytics purposes
- **Deleted data:** Permanently deleted within 30 days of deletion request

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Update

You can access and update your personal information at any time through the App's Profile section.

### Delete Your Account

You can delete your account at any time. This will:

- Permanently delete your account information
- Remove all your generated videos
- Delete your usage data
- Cannot be undone

To delete your account, go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

### Opt-Out of Data Collection

You can limit data collection by:

- **Denying camera permission:** You won't be able to use image-to-video features
- **Denying photo library permission:** You won't be able to upload reference images
- **Not using social features:** Your interactions won't be tracked

### Export Your Data

You can request a copy of your data by contacting us at privacy@sallymobile.app. We will provide your data in a machine-readable format within 30 days.

## Children's Privacy
